ABOUT

AshJr.

Director

Henri de Canecaude

Working as AshJr, Henri de Canecaude is a French filmmaker raised between London and Washington, D.C. Based in Paris, he works across narrative film, documentary and commercial projects.

His fiction explores moral uncertainty, inner conflict and the things people leave unsaid. Across his work, he is drawn to atmosphere, emotional detail and stories that stay with the viewer.

With a background in writing, music production and editing, he brings an attention to rhythm and sound to his filmmaking, from the first idea through to the final cut.
